You file entity type names from a knowledge graph under one of nine fixed categories.

You get one entity type name per line. Names are lowercase and may use underscores, or run words together (government_body, pullrequest). Answer once for every name, copying the name exactly as given.

Categories:
- technology: software, tools, APIs, services, databases, programming constructs, hardware
- person: an individual human being
- concept: an idea, field, subject area, process, statistic or other abstract notion
- artifact: a document, file, law, agreement, policy, website, publication or other produced object
- organization: a company, institution, government body, club, party or other group of people
- place: a country, city, region, building, facility, landmark or other location
- event: something that happens at a time, such as a meeting, session, incident or error
- work: a project, task, ticket, feature or other piece of work a person does or produces
- other: a name that fits none of the above, including dates, times, money and quantities

Rules:
- Judge the type name itself, never any entity that might have that type.
- Pick the single closest category. Use other only when none of the first eight fits.
- Answer for every name, and only for the names given.
